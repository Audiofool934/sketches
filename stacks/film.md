# film

Stack name in `brief.md`: `film`.

Open this note for an edited multi-scene film.
A loop that needs an MP4 stays on the stack that drew it and exports with the render contract in `CLAUDE.md`.
Playwright seeks `window.seek(t)`.
ffmpeg encodes.
That export is not a reason to change stacks.

Remotion (React timelines) and HyperFrames (HTML plus GSAP, then an MP4) are video-production tools.
Use one only when the brief asks for an edited multi-scene film and names that tool.
A 6-second loop does not need either.
Name the tool in `brief.md` before writing scenes.

A film is still a drawing with a clock.
Something new every 2 to 4 seconds.
A visible object in one scene becomes the first object of the next.
The last scene lands on a lockup or a held picture, and the type does not overshoot.

When the subject is a real product, use real screens, the real logo, and the real type.
Save those captures next to the piece and list them in the brief.
Do not invent a product UI.

Design the clock before the motion.
Voice and music are named in the brief.
Synthesized clicks, pops, thumps, and whooshes can live in the page.
A speaking voice or a sung part needs a separate audio model, named there.
Do not block the picture on that model.

The outside write-up of this shape is Hamza Khalid's brand-film setup: https://x.com/humzaakhalid/status/2105203643758895454
Take the scene order, the real screens, and the lockup.
Keep the toolkits out of this repo.
Read this note, not a folder of cloned examples.
