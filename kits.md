# Kits

These are outside references.
They are not dependencies.
Do not clone them into this repo.
Do not read the list before every piece.
Read one when the brief names that look, or when a film's motion is failing and the stack note is not enough.

## Drawn films

Read one, and only if the brief asks for that look.

- Hand-drawn canvas: https://github.com/alesha-pro/tools/tree/main/skills/hand-drawn-canvas-animation
- Anidoodle: https://github.com/alexgreensh/anidoodle
- JavaScript animation skills: https://github.com/iart-ai/javascript-animation-skills

A plain motion loop does not get a pack.

## Motion method

The render contract, the spring numbers, and the critique loop come from Movez's motion-studio course.
Course: https://x.com/0xMovez/status/2104216919033192746
Short card of the same method: https://x.com/0xMovez/status/2104576360119206296

Read the course when a film is failing, or when the brief asks for that method.
These notes already hold the part that applies to a piece.
Do not copy a one-liner, a prompt, or a repository from the course into a piece.
The lookalike reels are a warning.
A piece needs its own idea.

HyperFrames, Remotion, and the other engines named in the course stay outside this repo.
Read one only when the brief names that tool, and then only for `film`.

Free portions only:

- https://charliehills.substack.com/p/claude-code-motion-graphics (steps 1 and 2; the rest is paywalled)

Posts that match the render contract:
