# arrakis

## Idea

A desert planet in four pictures of stone, from a night under two moons to a city at dusk, each flowing into the next.
The viewer should feel the size and the heat of the desert, and the thing under it.

## Stack

mosaic.
Built with the `mosaic` skill, on a copy of the mosAIc engine as of 2026-10-07.

## Look

Palette: indigo and lilac for the night; spice orange, ochre, and rust for the day; a dark violet for the dusk.
Materials: limestone and terracotta for sand and flesh, basalt for rock and walls, marble for the teeth, glass for the skies, emit for the stars, the low sun, and the city's windows.
Banned: film stills, characters' likenesses, logos, and title type.

## Beats

0.0s night: dunes under two moons, stars, a spur of rock.
5.5s the stones fly to spice at dawn: a plume off the dunes, a winged flyer crossing.
11.5s the worm: it breaks the sand and rises, its round mouth ringed with teeth, a lone figure on the ridge.
17.5s the city at dusk: a stepped pyramid and leaning slab towers against a swollen sun.

## Motion

Duration: 26 seconds at 60 frames a second, 1920 by 1080 panels.
Each picture flows into the next out from its subject.
It fades in from black and out to black.

## Notes

Fan work drawn in code from the desert planet of Frank Herbert's *Dune*, with no frame, character, or design taken from any film.
The gallery on mosaic.audiofool.ai shows a silent web encode of `out/arrakis.mp4`.
